I am an Assistant Professor at the Purdue University Daniels school of Business. I am interested in research topics relating to media economics, behavioral industrial organization, and the intersection of quantitative marketing and public policy. My current work incorporates methodology ranging from structural modeling to machine learning, with an emphasis on unstructured data. 

I earned a Ph.D. in Marketing from Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management and a BA from the University of Chicago in Economics. I have previously also worked in the asset management industry as a research analyst at Dimensional Fund Advisors in Austin, TX. That was the only year of my life that I wasn't cold in the winter.
